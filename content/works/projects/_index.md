> 维护方式
> 1. 准备封面图：放图到 `static/image/works/projects/<slug>.png`（推荐 16:9）
> 2. 编辑 [data/projects.yaml](../../data/projects.yaml)，加一条：
>
>    ```yaml
>    - name: "项目名"
>      title: "English Subtitle"
>      desc: "衬线斜体描述"
>      cover: "/image/works/projects/<slug>.png"
>      href: "https://demo.example.com"   # 可空
>      repo: "https://github.com/..."     # 可空
>      tags: [tag1, tag2]
>      date: "2025-11"
>      featured: true                      # 可选：加 FEATURED 角标
>    ```
